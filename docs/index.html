Aura History Export

Export the browsing history available in your current profile to useful local files, with filters, previews, Google Takeout import, and an optional local archive.

Chrome Web Store Firefox Add-ons: review pending

Export the history you need

Choose the current day, a preset period, or a custom date range. Everything is processed inside the browser profile.

Flexible selection

Search records, include or exclude domains and protocols, hide localhost entries, and choose the columns to export.

Preview and organize

Preview the first 100 rows, group results by domain, and combine browser history with imported Takeout data.

Optional local archive

Keep new visits in IndexedDB for later exports and clear the archive at any time from the extension.

Export formats: XLSX CSV JSON DOCX TXT HTML

Your history stays local

Aura History Export has no analytics, telemetry, ads, remote code, or external service calls. History, imported files, settings, and archive data are not sent to the developer or third parties. Read the privacy policy.

Desktop browser support

Chromium-based browsers 121 or newer and Firefox Desktop 142 or newer.
